The moment you get your first job or open a bank account in India, two people will contact you: a friendly bank relationship manager and a well-meaning uncle.

Both will push the exact same product: an <Term id="endowment-policy">Endowment Policy</Term> or a <Term id="ulip">ULIP</Term>. They will tell you: *"Pay ₹50,000 every year for 10 years, get life insurance cover, and receive a guaranteed ₹10 Lakh lump sum tax-free!"*

It sounds like having your cake and eating it too. In reality, it is the single most destructive financial product marketed to young earners.

### The Golden Rule: Insurance is Protection, Not Investment

Insurance is meant to protect your financial dependents from catastrophic loss if you pass away prematurely. Investments are meant to grow your wealth faster than inflation.

When you mix the two into an endowment plan or ULIP:
1. **Your Life Cover is Abysmally Small:** A ₹50,000/year endowment plan usually offers barely ₹5 Lakh to ₹10 Lakh of life cover. If a breadwinner passes away, ₹5 Lakh does not even cover 2 years of rent and groceries for a family.
2. **Your Investment Return is Dismal:** After hefty agent commissions (often 20% to 35% of your first-year premium) and administrative charges, endowment policies return **4% to 5.5% per annum**. That is lower than real inflation.

### The Math: Pure Term vs Endowment Policy

Let us compare a 24-year-old earning ₹50,000/month:

| Parameter | Traditional Endowment / ULIP Plan | Pure Term Insurance + Index Fund |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Annual Outflow** | ₹60,000 per year | ₹10,000 (Term) + ₹50,000 (Index SIP) |
| **Life Insurance Cover** | ₹8,00,000 (₹8 Lakh) | **₹1,00,00,000 (₹1 Crore)** |
| **What happens if you die?** | Family gets ₹8 Lakh | **Family gets ₹1 Crore tax-free** |
| **What happens after 25 years?** | ~₹28 Lakh (at 4.5% returns) | **~₹68 Lakh** (at 12% index return) |
| **Outcome Verdict** | Low cover, low wealth | **12x higher protection, 2.4x more wealth** |

### What is Pure Term Life Insurance?

A <Term id="term-insurance">Term Insurance</Term> policy is pure risk cover, identical to car insurance.
- You pay an affordable premium (around ₹700 to ₹900 per month for a 24-year-old non-smoker).
- If you pass away during the policy term (say, until age 60 or 65), your family receives **₹1 Crore to ₹1.5 Crore** tax-free under Section 10(10D).
- If you survive the term, you get ₹0 back. 

Do not view the zero maturity return as a loss. You do not feel disappointed that your car did not crash so you could claim car insurance. The cheap premium frees up 85% of your money to invest aggressively in high-return index funds.

### 3 Rules for Buying Term Insurance
1. **Rule of Thumb Cover:** Take a sum assured equal to **15x to 20x your annual gross income**. If you earn ₹8 Lakh, buy at least ₹1.2 Crore to ₹1.5 Crore cover.
2. **Buy it Early:** Term insurance locks in your annual premium based on your age at entry. A policy bought at age 23 stays cheap for the entire 35-year tenure.
3. **Disclose Everything Honestly:** Always declare pre-existing medical conditions, smoking habits, or family medical histories. Under Section 45 of the Insurance Act, an insurer cannot dispute a policy after 3 years if initial disclosures were truthful.
