Imagine working hard for 10 years, saving ₹30 Lakh across bank fixed deposits and mutual fund accounts, and unexpectedly passing away in a road accident.

Your grieving parents or spouse visit the bank to access your savings to pay off urgent obligations, only to be told by the manager:
> *"The deceased account holder never added a nominee. You must obtain a Legal Heir Certificate or Succession Certificate from the District Civil Court before we can release ₹1."*

Obtaining a civil succession certificate in India typically takes **12 to 24 months** and costs tens of thousands in legal retainers.

All of this heartache can be avoided with a 5-minute online audit of your <Term id="nominee">Nominees</Term>.

### What is a Nominee in Indian Law?

A widespread myth in Indian personal finance is: *"If I make my brother the nominee of my bank account, the money belongs to him."*

Under Indian legal precedent established by the Supreme Court:
> **A nominee is a legal custodian/trustee, NOT the absolute legal owner of the assets (except in term life insurance under the Beneficial Nominee rule).**

The bank's only responsibility is to hand over the money to the registered nominee so the bank receives a valid legal discharge. The nominee must then distribute the money according to the deceased's legal will or Indian succession laws (such as the Hindu Succession Act or Indian Succession Act).

### The Beneficial Nominee Exception in Life Insurance
Under Section 39 of the Insurance Act (amended in 2015), if you name your **spouse, children, or parents** as nominees in your term insurance policy, they are legally classified as **Beneficial Nominees**. 

This means other distant legal heirs cannot claim a share of the insurance payout; 100% of the proceeds belong exclusively to those specified family members.

### The 5-Point Nominee Audit Checklist

Take 15 minutes this weekend to log in and verify nominees across every financial instrument:

| Financial Instrument | Where to Update | What You Need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bank Savings Accounts & FDs** | Net banking portal / Mobile app (Services → Nomination) | Nominee full legal name, relationship, DOB, and address |
| **Demat & Stock Trading Accounts** | Zerodha / Groww / AngelOne profile settings | PAN, Aadhaar/DOB, and allocation percentage (e.g., 50% Mother, 50% Father) |
| **Mutual Fund Folios** | MFCentral or CAMS / KFintech portal | Up to 3 nominees with percentage split |
| **Employee Provident Fund (EPF)** | Member e-Sewa portal (UAN Portal → Manage → E-Nomination) | Nominee photograph, Aadhaar number, and bank account |
| **Term & Health Insurance** | Insurance provider policy portal | Beneficial nominee selection with full contact details |

### What is a Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR)?

When selecting a life insurance company, look at the annual IRDAI report for its <Term id="claim-settlement-ratio">Claim Settlement Ratio</Term>.
- Choose an insurer with a CSR consistently **above 98%** over the past 3 to 5 financial years.
- Look at the **Average Settlement Duration** (how many days the insurer takes to disburse funds once claim documents are submitted).
