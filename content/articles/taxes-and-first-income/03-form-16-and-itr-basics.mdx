Mentioning the tax department creates immediate anxiety for most first-time earners. However, filing an <Term id="itr">Income Tax Return (ITR)</Term> in India has become almost entirely automated and takes under 20 minutes if your primary income is from salary.

Here is what every working professional needs to know.

### What is Form 16?

<Term id="form-16">Form 16</Term> is an official certificate issued by your employer by mid-June every year. It serves as legal evidence that the employer deducted TDS from your monthly salary and deposited it into the central government's treasury against your PAN.

Form 16 contains two parts:
1. **Part A:** Contains employer details, employee PAN, and a quarter-by-quarter breakdown of TDS deposited.
2. **Part B:** Contains your gross salary breakdown, allowances, chosen tax regime, deductions claimed, and net tax calculation.

### Why Must You File ITR Even If TDS Was Deducted?

A widespread misconception among fresh graduates is: *"My employer already deducted TDS from my paycheck, so my taxes are settled. Why should I file ITR?"*

Deducting TDS and filing an ITR are two different things:
- **TDS is an advance tax deduction.**
- **Filing an ITR is your official legal declaration** confirming your actual total earnings across all sources (salary, savings bank interest, fixed deposit interest, stock dividends, and freelance side projects).

#### 3 Essential Reasons You Must File:
1. **Claiming TDS Refunds:** If your employer deducted more tax than required, or if your income was below the taxable threshold, filing your ITR is the **only way** the government can refund the excess cash directly to your bank account.
2. **Visa Applications:** Embassies of countries like the US, UK, Schengen nations, and Canada require 2 to 3 years of ITR acknowledgments as proof of financial stability before approving travel visas.
3. **Home Loan Approvals:** Banks require past ITR forms when you apply for a vehicle loan or home mortgage.

### What is the AIS and Form 26AS?

Before clicking submit on the Income Tax Portal, always download your <Term id="ais">AIS (Annual Information Statement)</Term>.

The government's automated systems collect financial data linked to your PAN from banks, mutual funds, stock brokers, and credit card companies:
- Interest credited on your savings accounts
- Fixed deposit returns
- Stock or mutual fund sales (<Term id="ltcg">LTCG</Term> and <Term id="stcg">STCG</Term>)
- Dividend payouts

If your savings account earned ₹12,000 in interest and you fail to declare it in your ITR, the automated system triggers a mismatch inquiry notice. Verifying your AIS beforehand ensures 100% clean compliance.

### The 3 Step Filing Process for Salaried Beginners
1. Log in to `incometax.gov.in` using your PAN.
2. Select **ITR-1 (Sahaj)** for salaried income with one house property and income under ₹50 Lakh.
3. Review the pre-filled data, cross-check against your Form 16 and AIS, confirm your bank account for refund credit, and e-verify using an Aadhaar OTP.
