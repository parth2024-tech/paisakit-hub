You sign an offer letter for an "8 Lakh CTC", start your first full-time role, and eagerly await your first month's pay. You open your banking app on the 31st expecting ₹66,666, but see only ₹54,200 credited.

Where did the remaining ₹12,466 go?

Understanding the difference between <Term id="ctc">CTC</Term> and <Term id="in-hand-salary">In-Hand Salary</Term> prevents dangerous over-commitment on apartment leases and personal purchases.

### Breaking Down the Math

CTC stands for **Cost to Company**. It represents every single rupee the company spends to employ you for one year. 

A typical CTC package is structured into three distinct buckets:

```text
CTC = Direct Cash Allowances + Indirect Benefits + Statutory Retiral Benefits
```

#### 1. Direct Cash Components (Subject to Tax)
- **Basic Salary:** The foundational core of your compensation (typically 40% to 50% of total CTC). PF contributions and gratuity calculations are pegged to this figure.
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA):** Given to meet accommodation costs.
- **Special Allowance:** A balancing figure used by HR to make up the rest of the agreed CTC.

#### 2. Statutory Deductions (Deducted Before Transfer)
- **Employee Provident Fund (EPF):** Under Indian labor law, 12% of your Basic Salary is mandatorily deducted from your payslip and transferred into your EPFO account. The employer matches another 12% (part of which funds the Employee Pension Scheme).
- **Professional Tax (PT):** A state-level tax (typically ₹200 per month).
- **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS):** If your taxable income exceeds the rebate limit under the Income Tax Act, your employer estimates your annual liability and deducts a monthly installment upfront.

#### 3. Hidden or Non-Cash CTC Add-ons
Companies often inflate headline CTC numbers by including:
- Employer's matching PF contribution (3.67% EPF + 8.33% EPS)
- Gratuity allocation (~4.81% of basic salary, only payable after 5 continuous years of service)
- Group medical and term life insurance policy premiums
- Variable performance bonus (paid annually only if company milestones are met)

### Real-World Example: A ₹9,00,000 CTC

| Item | Monthly Amount | Annual Total | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Salary | ₹35,000 | ₹4,20,000 | Core salary |
| HRA | ₹14,000 | ₹1,68,000 | Accommodation |
| Special Allowance | ₹18,000 | ₹2,16,000 | Taxable cash |
| **Gross Monthly Pay** | **₹67,000** | **₹8,04,000** | Before deductions |
| Employee PF (12% of Basic) | -₹4,200 | -₹50,400 | Transferred to EPFO |
| Professional Tax | -₹200 | -₹2,400 | State deduction |
| Income Tax (TDS estimated) | -₹2,200 | -₹26,400 | Government tax |
| **Net In-Hand Bank Credit** | **₹60,400** | **₹7,24,800** | **What you actually receive** |

Notice that the remaining ~₹96,000 of the ₹9 Lakh CTC consists of the employer's matching PF, gratuity provision, and insurance cover.

### Golden Takeaway
When negotiating offers or planning rent budgets, always ask HR for the detailed **Salary Annexure / Payslip Projection**, and build your living budget exclusively around your verified net in-hand pay.
