House Rent Allowance (<Term id="hra">HRA</Term>) is one of the largest tax-saving provisions in Indian salary packages. Yet, thousands of young salaried professionals leave money on the table because they don't understand how the tax exemption formula actually works.

Here is the exact formula, the rules for claiming rent paid to parents, and how to stay 100% compliant.

### The 3-Part HRA Exemption Formula

Under Section 10(13A) of the Income Tax Act, your tax exemption is the **lowest of the following three calculations**:

1. **Actual HRA received** from your employer during the financial year.
2. **Actual rent paid minus 10% of your Basic Salary** (+ DA).
3. **50% of your Basic Salary** if you live in a metro city (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai), or **40% of Basic Salary** in any non-metro city (Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Gurgaon, etc.).

#### Worked Example:
- Basic Salary: ₹50,000 per month (₹6,00,000/year)
- HRA received: ₹20,000 per month (₹2,40,000/year)
- Rent paid in Bangalore (Non-Metro): ₹18,000 per month (₹2,16,000/year)

Now calculate the three conditions:
1. Actual HRA received: **₹2,40,000**
2. Rent paid minus 10% of Basic: ₹2,16,000 - ₹60,000 = **₹1,56,000**
3. 40% of Basic Salary: 40% of ₹6,00,000 = **₹2,40,000**

The lowest of the three is **₹1,56,000**. 
This ₹1,56,000 is completely exempt from income tax! The remaining ₹84,000 (₹2,40,000 - ₹1,56,000) is added to your taxable income.

### Can You Claim HRA While Living with Parents?

Yes, absolutely. The Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (ITAT) has upheld this in numerous judicial rulings, provided you fulfill legitimate legal requirements:

#### The 4 Non-Negotiable Rules to Pay Rent to Parents:
1. **Property Ownership:** The house must be legally registered in your parent's name (father or mother). You cannot be a co-owner of the property.
2. **Execute a Rent Agreement:** Draft a simple registered or notarized rent agreement between you and your parent specifying the monthly rent amount.
3. **Bank Transfer Proof:** Never pay in cash. Transfer the rent via net banking, UPI, or cheque every month so there is a clear banking audit trail.
4. **Parent Must Declare the Income:** Your parent must report this rental income under "Income from House Property" in their own annual ITR. 
   *(Pro-tip: If your parents are retired or have lower income, they receive a standard 30% municipal deduction on rental income, resulting in substantial net tax savings for the household).*

### What About the Landlord's PAN?
- If your annual rent paid exceeds **₹1,00,000** (more than ₹8,333 per month), your employer mandatorily requires the **PAN of the landlord**.
- If your landlord does not have a PAN, they must sign a formal declaration under Form 60.

### Note on Old vs New Tax Regime
HRA exemption is **only available under the Old Tax Regime**. The New Tax Regime does not allow any HRA deductions. If you choose the New Tax Regime, your entire HRA component is taxed at your regular slab rates.
