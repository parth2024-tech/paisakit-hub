If you deposit ₹1,00,000 into a standard savings bank account, log out, and return in 10 years, your bank passbook will show around ₹1,34,000.

It looks like you made ₹34,000 in profit. In reality, you are significantly poorer.

This mathematical paradox is caused by <Term id="inflation">Inflation</Term> — the silent tax that shrinks the purchasing power of idle money.

### What is Inflation?

Inflation is the gradual rate at which average prices for essential goods and services (food, housing, healthcare, transportation, tuition) rise over time.

Consider an everyday example:
- In 2014, a movie ticket in a metro city multiplex cost approximately **₹150**.
- In 2024, the exact same movie seat in the same hall costs **₹320**.

The movie did not get twice as long or twice as good; the currency simply lost half of its purchasing power.

### The Real Rate of Return Formula

Whenever you evaluate any place to keep money, calculate the **Real Rate of Return**:

```text
Real Return = Nominal Interest Rate - Inflation Rate - Tax Paid
```

Let us look at a typical Indian bank savings account:
- **Bank Savings Interest:** 3.0% per annum
- **Average Consumer Price Inflation:** 5.5% per annum
- **Net Real Return:** 3.0% - 5.5% = **-2.5% per year**

Every single calendar year your cash sits idle in that account, you lose roughly 2.5% of your real-world wealth. Over 15 years, that innocent-looking savings account cuts your purchasing power by nearly 35%.

### Where Different Assets Stand Against Inflation

To beat inflation over decades, your money must be placed into productive assets that grow faster than the cost of living:

| Asset Class | Long-Term Average Return | Real Return vs 5.5% Inflation | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Savings Bank Account | 2.5% – 3.5% | Negative (-2.5%) | Day-to-day spending only |
| 1-Year Bank Fixed Deposit | 6.5% – 7.0% | ~0.5% (Barely breaks even after tax) | Short-term goals (< 3 years) |
| Gold | 9% – 10% | +3.5% to +4.5% | Inflation hedge & cultural asset |
| Equity / <Term id="index-fund">Index Funds</Term> | 12% – 14% | **+6.5% to +8.5%** | **Long-term wealth creation (> 5 years)** |

### What Should You Do With Your Money?

1. **Keep 1 Month of Expenses in Savings:** For UPI payments, rent, food, and grocery bills.
2. **Keep 3–6 Months in an Emergency Buffer:** In a sweep FD or liquid fund.
3. **Deploy Everything Else into Productive Assets:** Use an automated <Term id="sip">SIP</Term> into broad market index funds to ensure your hard-earned money outpaces the rising cost of living.
