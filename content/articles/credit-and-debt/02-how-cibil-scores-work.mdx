When you apply for a credit card, car loan, or home mortgage, the lending manager does not ask what college you went to or how many followers you have on social media. 

They pull your <Term id="cibil-score">CIBIL Score</Term>. 

A strong score saves you tens of lakhs over your lifetime through preferential interest rates. A degraded score can get your loan applications rejected outright.

### The Score Breakdown (300 to 900)

- **300 to 650 (Poor / High Risk):** Frequent defaults or high delinquency. Almost impossible to get unsecured credit.
- **650 to 749 (Fair):** May get approved, but banks will charge higher interest margins and demand co-signers.
- **750 to 900 (Excellent):** Prime borrower. Fast approvals, waived processing fees, and rock-bottom interest rates.

### The 5 Factors That Calculate Your Score

TransUnion CIBIL and other bureaus (Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark) use proprietary algorithms based on five core pillars:

```text
Credit Score = Repayment History (35%) + Credit Utilization (30%) + Credit Age (15%) + Credit Mix (10%) + Recent Inquiries (10%)
```

#### 1. Repayment History (35% Weight)
Did you pay all your credit card bills and loan EMIs on time, every single month? Even a 5-day delay reported as past-due can leave a black mark on your record for up to 36 months.

#### 2. Credit Utilization Ratio (30% Weight)
The proportion of your revolving credit limit that you spend. If your total credit card limit across all cards is ₹1,00,000, and your monthly statement balance is ₹60,000, your utilization is 60%.
**The Rule:** Keep your utilization **consistently below 30%**. If you spend heavily, request a higher limit or pay down part of the balance before the billing cycle closes.

#### 3. Age of Credit History (15% Weight)
How long have your credit accounts been open? Older accounts prove sustained reliability.
**Tip:** Never cancel your oldest credit card even if you rarely use it; keep it alive with an occasional small utility recharge.

#### 4. Credit Mix (10% Weight)
A healthy balance between secured loans (home loan, auto loan) and unsecured credit (credit cards, personal loans) demonstrates broad repayment competence.

#### 5. Recent Credit Inquiries (10% Weight)
Applying for three credit cards and a personal loan within a two-week span triggers multiple "Hard Inquiries" from lenders, signaling financial desperation to the algorithm.

### How to Check Your Score for Free
Under RBI regulations, every Indian citizen is entitled to **one free full credit report per bureau every calendar year**. 
Checking your own score on official bureau portals or approved banking apps is a **soft inquiry** and will never decrease your score by even a fraction of a point.
