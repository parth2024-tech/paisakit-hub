If you keep ₹80,000 in a standard savings account, your bank pays you roughly 2.7% to 3.0% annual interest. Meanwhile, that same bank lends out your money to corporate borrowers at 9% to 12%.

You do not need to let your money sit lazy. By activating a feature called <Term id="auto-sweep">Auto-Sweep</Term> (also called Multi-Option Deposit or 2-in-1 Account), you can earn fixed deposit interest of **6.5% to 7.2%** while enjoying 100% instant liquid access.

### How Auto-Sweep Works Under the Hood

Auto-sweep links your savings bank account to a virtual pool of short-term fixed deposits.

1. **Threshold Limit:** You designate a minimum operating balance (for instance, ₹25,000).
2. **Sweep-In:** Whenever your balance crosses ₹25,000 (say, your salary of ₹75,000 gets credited), the excess ₹50,000 is automatically carved into fixed deposit units (typically in multiples of ₹1,000 or ₹5,000) earning full FD rates.
3. **Sweep-Out (Reverse Sweep):** If you make an online rent payment of ₹35,000 via UPI or debit card, the bank checks your liquid ₹25,000, automatically breaks only ₹10,000 worth of FD units to cover the difference, and completes your transfer instantly.

There is **zero manual intervention**, zero paper forms, and no bounce penalty.

### Bank Terminology Across Major Indian Banks

Different banks market auto-sweep under slightly different brand names:

| Bank | Feature Name | Minimum Threshold | FD Tenure Pool |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **State Bank of India (SBI)** | Multi Option Deposit (MOD) | ₹35,000 (sweeps in units of ₹1,000) | 1 to 5 years |
| **HDFC Bank** | Sweep-in / Super Saver | ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 | 1 year (auto-renewing) |
| **ICICI Bank** | Money Multiplier | ₹10,000 above MAB | 1 year |
| **Kotak Mahindra Bank** | ActivMoney | ₹25,000 | 180 days (earns ~7.0%) |

### How to Turn It On in 2 Minutes
1. Log in to your bank's official mobile application or net banking portal.
2. Navigate to **Fixed Deposits / Deposits Section**.
3. Select **"Auto-Sweep / Multi-Option Deposit / Money Multiplier"**.
4. Choose your linked savings account and select your threshold balance (₹25,000 is ideal for early-career earners).
5. Confirm with your transaction PIN or OTP.

### The Tax Angle: What You Must Know
Interest earned on standard savings accounts is tax-exempt up to ₹10,000 per financial year under **Section 80TTA**. However, interest earned from auto-sweep FDs is categorized as regular fixed deposit interest and does not qualify for Section 80TTA exemption. It is added to your total income and taxed according to your applicable slab rate. 

Even after taxes, earning 6.5% net of tax is vastly superior to losing purchasing power at 2.7% in a sleepy savings account.
