The <Term id="upi">UPI</Term> infrastructure created by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) has made digital transactions frictionless. However, fraudsters exploit psychological urgency rather than technical bugs to drain accounts.

Understanding one inviolable technical rule eliminates 95% of digital banking scams.

### Rule 1: A UPI PIN Is Only Required to Deduct Money

This is the golden law of Indian digital banking:
> **You NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN, scan a QR code, or approve a biometric prompt to receive money.**

Whenever you enter your 4-digit or 6-digit PIN on Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM, money is **leaving** your bank balance. If a buyer, lottery claim, customer support representative, or recruiter claims they are sending you funds and asks you to "input your PIN to verify receiving", they are attempting to debit your funds immediately.

### Common UPI Scam Patterns to Recognize

#### 1. The OLX / Marketplace Reverse QR Scam
You list an old bicycle or monitor for ₹4,000 on an online classifieds site. A buyer contacts you saying they work for the army or an enterprise, agrees to your asking price without bargaining, and sends a screenshot of a QR code saying: *"Scan this QR code in Google Pay and click pay to accept ₹4,000 into your account."*
When you scan and confirm, your account is instantly debited for ₹4,000.

#### 2. The Unsolicited Collect Request
Fraudsters send a collect request of ₹2,000 or ₹10,000 disguised with names like "Recharge Refund" or "Cashback Voucher". People absentmindedly click notification banners and approve it without reading the confirmation prompt.

#### 3. Fake Customer Care Numbers
Never search Google for "Customer Care Number for XYZ Payment App". Scammers create bogus Google Maps listings and SEO pages with their phone numbers. Legitimate banking apps provide verified in-app grievance tickets.

### How to Protect Yourself
- **Set Daily Limits:** Keep your daily UPI limit capped at ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 inside your banking app settings. Increase it manually only when paying tuition fees or major bills.
- **Maintain a Primary vs Secondary Account:** Keep your substantial savings in an account that does not have UPI activated, and maintain a secondary account with ₹10,000–₹15,000 for everyday grocery and cafe swipes.
- **Report Immediately:** If scammed, call 1930 (the National Cyber Crime Helpline) within the golden hour to freeze fund routing before fraudsters cash out at ATMs.
